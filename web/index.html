Claude Desktop Buddy

Flash the ESP32 desk-pet firmware straight from your browser — no PlatformIO, no toolchain. Works on a M5StickC Plus.

Your browser can't talk to serial devices. Open this page in desktop Chrome, Edge, or Opera.

Before you start

  1. Use desktop Chrome, Edge, or Opera (Web Serial is required).
  2. Connect the M5StickC Plus to your computer over USB-C.
  3. Click Install firmware and pick the device's serial port.
  4. Choose Install and wait for the flash to finish.

Re-flashing an already-used device? The installer offers an erase option — pick it for a clean factory state. You can also factory reset from the device: hold A → settings → reset → factory reset → tap twice.

After flashing

Pair the buddy with Claude by enabling developer mode (Help → Troubleshooting → Enable Developer Mode), then open Developer → Open Hardware Buddy… and click Connect. Full instructions are in the README.
